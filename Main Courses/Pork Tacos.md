# Pork Tacos

Source: Brian Lagerstrom

## Ingredients

- 5 lb. pork shoulder, cut into 3 inch cubes, bone removed
- 3 tbsp. kosher salt
- 1 tbsp. dried oregano
- 1 tbsp. onion powder
- 1 tbsp. garlic powder
- 1 tbsp. black pepper
- EVOO
- Chicken stock
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 3 bay leaves
- 1 orange, halved and deseeded
  - ALTERNATIVE: 2 limes, halved and deseeded
- 1/4 cup rendered pork fat, reserved from cooking
- 1 cup pork liquid, reserved from cooking
- OPTIONAL: 3-4 tbsp. lime juice
- Serve with: warm tortillas, white onion, cilantro, pickled onions, salsa

## Prep 1

- Combine pork with salt, oregano, onion & garlic powder, black pepper in large bowl
- Refrigerate 2-8 hours to cure

## Prep 2

- Heat EVOO in Dutch oven over med-high heat
- Sear pork to get some extra flavor
- Pack pork in bottom of pot with cinnamon stick, bay leaves, orange
- Add enough stock to almost cover the pork
- Bring to a boil
- Cover and simmer for 3 hours until almost fork tender
- Remove from heat and let rest, covered, for 1 hour
- Move to fridge and let sit 6-8 hours (or overnight)

## Directions

- Move pot to the stove top
- Remove pork pieces from the pot and set aside
- Remove the rendered fat and set aside
- Remove the pork liquid (gelatinized now) and set aside
- Add lime juice, if using it
- Return pork to pot and gently shred by hand
- Mix about 1/4 cup fat and 1 cup pork liquid with pork by hand
  - Add more, if needed
- Heat pot on med-low for about 30 minutes until warmed through
- OPTIONAL: crisp up the pork in a non-stick pan over med-high heat for carnitas
- Serve with tortillas and toppings
