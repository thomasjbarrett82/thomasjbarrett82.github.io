# Coleslaw

Source: Serious Eats

## Ingredients

- 3-4 pound head of green cabbage, finely shredded
- 1 red onion, finely sliced
- 1 large carrot, peeled and shredded
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 cup + 3 tbsp. white sugar
- 1/2 cup kosher salt
- 1 cup mayo
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 2 tbsp. Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp. black pepper

## Prep

- Combine cabbage, onion, carrot, parsley, 1 cup sugar, kosher salt in large bowl
- Let rest 5-10 minutes
- Transfer to large colander and rinse under cold water
- Dry with salad spinner or blotting with towels in sheet pan
- Return to large bowl

## Directions

- Whisk mayo, vinegar, mustard, pepper, 3 tbsp. sugar in bowl
- Stir dressing into cabbage mixture
- Taste and adjust seasoning as desired
- Rest in fridge for 1+ hour before serving
