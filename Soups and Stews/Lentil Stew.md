# Lentil Stew

Source: Derek Sarno

This recipe is pretty loose, adapt with whatever you want or have on hand.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups lentils, washed and drained
- 6 cups water
- 3-4 tbsp. cooking oil
- 1 onion, peeled and diced
- 5-6 cloves garlic, peeled and minced
- Spices and seasoning, ~4 tbsp. total
  - Use whatever sounds good: cumin, curry, Italian mix, smoked paprika, etc.
- OPTIONAL: quick cooking veggies, ~2 cups total
- 1 lime, zested and juiced
- Salt & pepper

## Directions

- Combine lentils and water in large bowl, set aside
- Heat stew pot and cooking oil over medium
- Add onion and cook, stirring, for 5 minutes
- Add garlic and cook for another 2-3 minutes
- Add spices and bloom for ~1 minute
- Add lentils & water to the pot, deglazing if needed
- Add any veggies, if using them
- Bring to a boil and then cover
- Reduce heat to low and simmer for ~15 minutes until lentils are tender
- Add the lime juice and stir
- Let sit for 10 minutes before serving
