# Rice

## Rice Cooker Time Table

|Rice Type|Cooking Time|Water Ratio|Texture|
|---------|------------|-----------|-------|
|White long grain|15–25 min|1:1.25|Soft & fluffy|
|Jasmine rice|15–20 min|Slightly less water|Light, aromatic|
|Basmati rice|15–22 min|1:1.25|Separate grains|
|Sushi / short grain|18–25 min|1:1.2|Sticky|
|Brown rice|35–50 min|1:1.75|Chewy|
|Wild rice blend|45–60 min|1:2|Firm|

## Stovetop

### White Rice

- Rinse the rice
- Use 1.5:1 ratio of water:rice in a pot
- Bring to boil over med-high heat
- Turn to low, cover and simmer for 20 mins
- Remove from heat for 5 minutes before fluffing with fork

Optional: use broth instead of water for more flavor

### Brown Rice

- Same as white rice, but use 2:1 water:rice ratio
